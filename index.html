ABOUT

A calmer way to plan

Calendar Assistant turns everyday sentences like “exercise four times a week” or “hang out with friends on Saturday” into events on a real calendar.

Weekdays, dates, frequencies and conflicts are worked out by a scheduling engine, not guessed. Before anything is saved you see exactly what will be added, moved or removed, and you decide.

Your tasks stay on your device, and sync across your devices when you open the page signed in to Claude. Edit any event, check it off, and review your week with a recap.

FAQ

Questions

What is Calendar Assistant?

A personal planner that turns goals, tasks and commitments into a schedule, with a week and month calendar, a daily progress ring, a morning briefing and a weekly recap.

How does AI planning work?

You describe your week in plain language. The request is read into activities, dates and weekdays are calculated in code, existing events are checked for conflicts, and you get a preview to approve. Claude helps interpret vague goals when it is available.

Can I create recurring activities?

Yes. “Exercise 4 times a week” spreads sessions across different days. “Every Monday, Wednesday and Friday” uses exactly those days. “Every day” and “every weekday” work too.

Can I edit my calendar?

Yes. Click any event to change its title, date, time or duration, mark it complete, or delete it. You can also say “move exercise to Wednesday” or “remove studying on Friday” in the planner.

Is Calendar Assistant free?

There is no sign-up form or payment step in this app. Saving across devices uses your Claude account.

Does the AI understand natural language?

It understands many everyday phrasings. When something is unclear it tells you what it couldn’t schedule instead of guessing.
